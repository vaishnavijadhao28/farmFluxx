Ginger Powder

✅ What it is

  • Made by drying fresh ginger roots and grinding them into a fine powder.
  • Warm, pungent, aromatic flavor.
  • Used in cooking, herbal medicine, teas, and wellness products.

🍴 Culinary Uses

  • Adds flavor to curries, soups, stews, and marinades.
  • Used in ginger tea, masala chai, and herbal drinks.
  • Baking: gingerbread, cookies, cakes.
  • Pickles, spice blends (garam masala, chai masala).

🌿 Health Benefits

  • Boosts digestion & metabolism.
  • Natural remedy for cold, cough, sore throat.
  • Reduces inflammation & joint pain.
  • Helps with nausea and motion sickness.
  • Supports immunity.

📦 Industrial / Wellness Uses.

  • Herbal teas & nutraceuticals.
  • Ayurvedic medicines.
  • Skincare (anti-inflammatory packs, scrubs).

☕ Ginger Tea (Adrak Chai / Herbal Tea)

Ingredients:

  • 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tsp tea leaves / green tea
  • Honey or jaggery (to taste)

Method :

  • 1. Boil water with ginger powder for 5 minutes.
  • 2. Add tea leaves & simmer.
  • 3. Strain into a cup.
  • 4. Add honey/jaggery & serve hot.

🌿 Ginger Kadha (Immunity Drink)

Ingredients:

  • 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 2 cups water
  • 5–6 tulsi (basil) leaves
  • 4–5 black peppercorns
  • Small stick cinnamon
  • Honey/jaggery (optional)

Method :

  • 1. Boil water with ginger powder & spices.
  • 2. Reduce to almost half.
  • 3. Strain & serve warm.
  • 4. Take daily for immunity.

🍪 Ginger Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup wheat flour
  • 2 tbsp ginger powder
  • ½ cup jaggery powder
  • 2 tbsp butter / ghee
  • ½ tsp baking powder

Method :

  • 1. Mix flour, ginger powder, jaggery & baking powder.
  • 2. Add butter/ghee & make a dough.
  • 3. Shape into cookies & bake at 180°C for 12–15 mins.
  • 4. Cool & enjoy with tea.

Banana powder

(especially green/raw banana powder) is a versatile ingredient with many culinary, health, and industrial uses. Here are the main ones:

🍴 Culinary Uses

  • 1. Baby food – Easily digestible, often used as a weaning food.
  • 2. Thickening agent – Works in soups, gravies, and smoothies.
  • 3. Gluten-free baking – Used in cakes, bread, pancakes, and cookies.
  • 4. Snacks & mixes – Added to porridges, energy bars, and laddoos.
  • 5. Smoothies & shakes – Natural sweetener and thickener.
  • 6. Breakfast cereals – Mix with oats, millet, or cornflakes.

🌱 Health & Nutrition Uses

  • 1. Rich in resistant starch – Helps manage diabetes and weight.
  • 2. Gut health – Prebiotic effect, supports digestion.
  • 3. Electrolyte balance – Natural source of potassium and magnesium.
  • 4. Easily digestible energy – Great for athletes and kids.

🏭 Industrial Uses

  • 1. Rich in resistant starch – Helps manage diabetes and weight.
  • 2. Gut health – Prebiotic effect, supports digestion.
  • 3. Electrolyte balance – Natural source of potassium and magnesium.
  • 4. Easily digestible energy – Great for athletes and kids.

✅ Highlights

  • 🌱 Gluten-Free & Vegan
  • ⚡ Rich in Potassium
  • 🍌 Natural Energy Booster
  • 🌿 Good for Digestion

🥣 Banana Powder Porridge (for kids & adults)

  • 2 tbsp banana powder
  • 1 cup milk / water
  • 1 tsp jaggery / honey (optional)
  • Cook until smooth & serve warm

🥞 Banana Powder Pancakes

  • 1 cup wheat flour + 2 tbsp banana powder
  • Add milk, jaggery & baking powder
  • Cook on pan → serve with honey/fruit

🥤 Banana Powder Smoothie

  • 1 tbsp banana powder
  • 1 banana + 1 glass milk / almond milk
  • Blend with honey / dry fruits
  • 4–5 black peppercorns
  • Serve chilled & refreshing

Jackfruit powder

It is a nutritious plant-based product made by dehydrating ripe or raw jackfruit pulp and grinding it into a fine powder. 🌱 It’s gaining popularity as a healthy alternative to refined flours and as a functional ingredient in food and beverages.

🌿 Health Benefits:

  • 1. Regulates blood sugar (especially raw jackfruit powder).
  • 2. Good source of fiber, potassium, and antioxidants.
  • 3. Boosts immunity & gut health.
  • 4. Gluten-free, vegan, and natural.

🍴 Uses in Daily Diet:

  • 1. Add 1–2 tbsp to roti flour mix.
  • 2. Blend into smoothies or shakes.
  • 3. Mix with porridge, idli/dosa batter, or soups.
  • 4. Use as a base for cakes, cookies, or pancakes.

🍪 Jackfruit Powder Roti

  • Mix wheat flour + jackfruit powder (3:1 ratio)
  • Add water & knead dough
  • Roll into rotis & cook on tawa
  • Healthy, low-GI flatbread

🥤 Jackfruit Smoothie

  • 1 tbsp jackfruit powder
  • 1 glass milk (or plant milk)
  • 1 banana + honey
  • Blend & serve chilled

🥞 Jackfruit Pancakes

  • 1 cup jackfruit powder + wheat flour
  • Add milk, jaggery, baking powder
  • Cook on pan, serve with honey or fruit

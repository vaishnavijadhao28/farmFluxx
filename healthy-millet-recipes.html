🍲 Foxtail Millet Recipes


1️⃣ Foxtail Millet Upma

  • Ingredients : Foxtail millet, onion, green chili, ginger, curry leaves, carrot, beans, peas.
  • Method : Roast millet lightly, cook with sautéed veggies & spices, add water and steam until fluffy.
  • Highlight : Light breakfast, high in fiber.

2️⃣ Foxtail Millet Khichdi

  • Ingredients : Foxtail millet, moong dal, turmeric, ginger, cumin, ghee.
  • Method :Pressure cook millet & dal together with spices.
  • Highlight : Easy to digest, comfort food for all ages

3️⃣ Foxtail Millet Dosa

  • Ingredients : Foxtail millet, moong dal, turmeric, ginger, cumin, ghee.
  • Method : Pressure cook millet & dal together with spices.
  • Highlight : Easy to digest, comfort food for all ages

4️⃣ Foxtail Millet Pulao

  • Ingredients : Foxtail millet, mixed vegetables, garam masala, ginger-garlic paste.
  • Method : Cook millet separately, sauté veggies & spices, mix & simmer.
  • Highlight : Wholesome lunchbox meal.

5️⃣ Foxtail Millet Payasam (Kheer)

  • Ingredients : Foxtail millet, milk (or coconut milk), jaggery, cardamom, cashew, raisins..
  • Method :Cook millet in milk, add jaggery & flavorings, garnish with nuts.
  • Highlight :Traditional sweet, rich in nutrients.

🍲 Finger Millet (Ragi) Recipes


1️⃣ Ragi Porridge (Ragi Malt)

  • Ingredients : Ragi flour, water, milk, jaggery, cardamom.
  • Method : Cook ragi flour in water, add milk & jaggery, simmer until smooth.
  • Highlight : Excellent baby food, calcium-rich.

2️⃣ Ragi Roti

  • Ingredients : Ragi flour, onion, green chili, coriander leaves, cumin seeds.
  • Method :Mix flour with warm water & veggies, flatten into rotis, cook on tava.
  • Highlight : High-fiber, keeps you full longer.

3️⃣ Ragi Dosa

  • Ingredients : Ragi flour, rice flour, curd, green chili, ginger.
  • Method : Make a thin batter, ferment if desired, cook into crisp dosas.
  • Highlight : Gluten-free breakfast alternative.

4️⃣ Ragi Idli

  • Ingredients : Ragi flour, urad dal, idli rava.
  • Method : Mix with fermented dal batter, steam in idli molds.
  • Highlight : Soft & fluffy, diabetic-friendly.

5️⃣ Ragi Ladoo

  • Ingredients : Ragi flour, jaggery, ghee, nuts.
  • Method :Roast flour, mix with jaggery syrup & ghee, shape into laddoos.
  • Highlight :Energy-boosting, healthy sweet snack.

🍲 Pearl Millet (Bajra) Recipes

  • Highlight : Traditional staple, rich in fiber & iron.

  • Ingredients : Bajra grains (crushed), moong dal, turmeric, cumin, ghee.

  • Highlight : Nutritious one-pot meal, good for digestion.

3️⃣ Bajra Upma

  • Ingredients : Bajra grains, onion, green chili, curry leaves, vegetables.
  • Method : Roast bajra, cook with sautéed veggies & spices until fluffy.
  • Highlight : Healthy breakfast alternative to rava upma.

  • Method : Cook bajra flour in milk or buttermilk, add seasoning or jaggery.
  • Highlight : Energy-packed meal, suitable for all ages.

5️⃣ Bajra Ladoo

  • Ingredients : Bajra flour, jaggery, ghee, nuts.
  • Method : Roast flour, mix with jaggery syrup & ghee, roll into ladoos.
  • Highlight : Healthy winter sweet, boosts stamina.

🍲 Makhana (Foxnut) Recipes


1️⃣ Roasted Makhana Snack

  • Ingredients : Makhana, ghee/oil, salt, black pepper, turmeric (optional).
  • Method : Roast makhana in ghee/oil until crisp, sprinkle spices.
  • Highlight : Healthy tea-time snack, crunchy & light.

2️⃣ Makhana Kheer

  • Ingredients : Makhana, milk, jaggery/sugar, cardamom, cashew, raisins.
  • Method : Roast makhana, simmer in milk, add jaggery, garnish with nuts.
  • Highlight : Festive dessert, calcium-rich.

3️⃣ Makhana Curry

  • Ingredients : Makhana, onion, tomato, ginger-garlic paste, spices, cream.
  • Method : Roast makhana, cook in rich tomato-onion gravy with spices.
  • Highlight : Royal-style main dish, pairs with roti/rice.

4️⃣ Makhana Chaat

  • Ingredients : Roasted makhana, onion, tomato, green chili, coriander, lemon juice, chaat masala.
  • Method : Toss everything together and serve fresh.
  • Highlight : Nutritious, tangy evening snack.

5️⃣ Makhana Ladoo

  • Ingredients : Roasted makhana, jaggery, ghee, dry fruits.
  • Method : Powder makhana, mix with jaggery syrup & ghee, shape into ladoos.
  • Highlight : Energy-boosting sweet, perfect for kids.

🍲 Jowar Recipes (Sorghum)


1️⃣ Jowar Roti (Sorghum Flatbread)

  • Ingredients : Jowar flour, warm water, pinch of salt.
  • Method : Mix flour & salt, knead with warm water into dough, roll thin rotis, cook on hot tawa until golden spots appear.
  • Highlight : Served hot with sabzi or dal.

2️⃣ Jowar Upma

  • Ingredients : Jowar rava, onion, green chili, curry leaves, mustard seeds, water, oil.
  • Method : Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds, add curry leaves & chili, sauté onion, roast Jowar rava, add water & cook fluffy.
  • Highlight : Light & healthy breakfast, best with chutney.

3️⃣ Jowar Vegetable Khichdi

  • Ingredients : Jowar grains (soaked), moong dal, mixed vegetables, cumin, ginger, turmeric, water.
  • Method : Pressure cook Jowar grains, sauté cumin, ginger & veggies, add dal & Jowar, cook until khichdi consistency.
  • Highlight : Nutritious one-pot meal, goes well with curd or pickle.

🍲 Proso Millet Khichdi


1️⃣ Proso Millet Khichdi

  • Ingredients : Proso Millet, Moong Dal, onion, tomato, carrot/beans/peas, green chili, ginger, cumin seeds, turmeric, salt, ghee/oil, water, fresh coriander.
  • Method : Wash & soak millet + dal, sauté cumin, chili, ginger, onion, tomato & veggies, add spices + millet & dal, cook with water till soft, garnish with coriander.
  • Highlight : Protein-rich, gluten-free comfort meal – great for diabetics & weight watchers.
